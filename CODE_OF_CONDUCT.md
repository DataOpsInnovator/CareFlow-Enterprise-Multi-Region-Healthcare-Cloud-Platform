# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a professional, respectful and inclusive engineering environment.

Expected behavior includes:

- communicating constructively and respectfully;
- focusing technical criticism on ideas and implementations, not people;
- assuming good intent while remaining precise about engineering risk;
- protecting confidential information and credentials;
- welcoming questions and differing technical perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other unacceptable behavior are not tolerated.

For concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the security/contact mechanism documented in `SECURITY.md`.
